Turning 18: staff cadet or CFAV

When you turn 18 you can stay on as a staff cadet or become an adult volunteer (CFAV). Start the conversation a few months before your birthday.

  1. Talk to the OC or Adjutant a few months before your 18th birthday.
  2. Choose your route: staff cadet or CFAV.
  3. Complete the paperwork and checks for your route.
  4. Complete the required training.
  5. Get confirmed in your new role.
