O18

Becoming a Staff Cadet

Are you 17 years and 3 months or older? To stay in the Air Cadets after your 18th birthday, you need to become a Staff Cadet. That means completing some checks, forms and training first. This guide walks you through each step.

Important: at the very least, your DBS and Safeguarding training must be complete before your 18th birthday, or you can't stay on. Please don't leave it until the last minute.

What you need to do

  1. DBS application, through U-Check
  2. BPSS forms
  3. Safeguarding Induction, on RAFAC Learn
  4. AVIP course
  5. Mandatory learning and agreements
1 DBS application

Email Wing HQ and ask to be added to the U-Check portal so your DBS application can be started.

Send to
RAFAC-LASER-AllUsers-Pers@mod.gov.uk
CC
Adj.31@rafac.mod.gov.uk (Squadron Adjutant)
Subject
U-Check / DBS Portal Request

Copy this message and replace the parts in square brackets with your own details:

I, [your full name], give permission for RAFAC WHQ to add me to the U-Check portal.

My personal email address is provided below:

Email: [your personal email address]
Full Name: [your full name]

Use a personal email address that you can always get into, not a school one.

2 BPSS forms

You can complete the BPSS forms digitally or on paper.

Download the blank BPSS form (Word)

Fill it in using Word on your phone or computer, save it, then email it to Adj.31@rafac.mod.gov.uk. Don't send it through WhatsApp or social media, because it contains your personal details.

Prefer paper? Ask Fg Off Makwana-Mehmi or CI Ciftci for a copy. Either way, return it as soon as you can.

3 Safeguarding Induction
  1. Log in to RAFAC Learn.
  2. Select Course Explorer.
  3. Search for Safeguarding Induction and open the course.
  4. Complete every section.

Check that the course shows as completed when you've finished.

4 AVIP course

You don't need to arrange this yourself. CI Ciftci or Fg Off Makwana-Mehmi will book you onto an available course. Once you have a date, make sure you attend.

5 Mandatory learning and agreements

All Staff Cadets must also complete these squadron requirements:

  1. Cadet Code of Conduct. In Cadet Portal, go to Agreements, then Cadet Code of Conduct. Read and accept it.
  2. Prevent Awareness, Course 1 only. Open the Prevent Awareness course.
  3. Responsible for Information, on RAFAC Learn.
  4. Safeguarding Awareness, on RAFAC Learn.
  5. Security Advice Brief, on RAFAC Learn.
  6. Staff Cadet Service Agreement. A paper form to print, complete and sign. Ask CI Ciftci or Fg Off Makwana-Mehmi for a copy.

Your checklist

Tick things off as you go. Your ticks are saved on this device only, so let the Adj team know when you finish each one.

Stuck on a course, form or system? Speak to Fg Off Makwana-Mehmi or CI Ciftci.
